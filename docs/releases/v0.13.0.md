0.13.0 is a minor release that reworks Settings and the header, and keeps your own settings out of git. Settings opens in a pop-up over the whole panel, an agent's models show as soon as you choose it, and the view stays still as you change things. Light and dark move into Settings, and the header shows the dot-matrix "speck" word again, beside the base branch. Who builds and reviews your tickets, and how, is now saved for your machine without a commit, and new tickets follow those settings. It matters because Settings is where you choose the agents that build and review your work, and choosing them shouldn't crowd the panel, jump about, or leave a commit to ship.

## What's new

- **Settings in a pop-up.** Settings covers the whole panel, with its settings in a centred column, rather than a narrow pane down the right. Close, Escape or a click beside the column closes it, asking first if you have unsaved changes.
- **Models beside their agent.** The builder, the reviewer and the Foreman each have one row: the agent, then its model. Choosing another agent fills its model list straight away, without saving and opening Settings again.
- **A still view.** Changing a setting leaves the view where you scrolled it, and an explanation floats over its row instead of pushing the rest down. On a phone, Settings no longer slides sideways.
- **Light and dark in Settings, and speck's name back in the header.** The light/dark choice is in Settings' new Look group. The header shows "speck" in dot-matrix lettering at every width, then the base branch, such as "base main". The Foreman's notice board scrolls at a third of its old speed, so you can read it.
- **Your own settings stay on your machine.** Who builds and reviews, their models and effort, `auto_ship`, the Foreman and how many builds and test runs go at once are saved for this machine by `speck config set` and the panel's Save, with no commit and nothing to ship.
- **Tickets follow your settings.** New tickets don't write down their builder or reviewer, so changing the reviewer reaches every open ticket that leaves it blank. A ticket in review names the model reviewing it, such as "grok-4.7-high is reviewing · round 2".
- **Sturdier reviews.** A reviewer that writes a line of prose around its verdict is recorded rather than stopping the build, and a review round longer than 9 minutes is waited for rather than cut off.

## Update

Paste the setup prompt from the README into your coding agent, or run this in your project's folder:

```sh
npx github:luroai/speck#v0.13.0 init
```

## Upgrading from 0.12.0

- Run `npx github:luroai/speck#v0.13.0 init` again in each project to update the speck command and skills. It keeps your config and tickets.
- Light and dark have moved from the header to the Look group in Settings. The header shows "speck" and "base <branch>" rather than "<project> · base <branch>".
- `speck config set` and the panel's Save now save your own settings for this machine, in `speck/.cache/machine/config.yml`, which git ignores, and no longer change `speck/config.yml` for them. The values already in `speck/config.yml` stay as the defaults for anyone without their own. To change one of those defaults for everyone, run `speck config set --repo <key> <value>` and commit `speck/config.yml`. The panel can't change them any more.
- New tickets leave their builder and reviewer to your settings. Tickets made before this release still name theirs and keep them: clear one with `speck set <id> reviewer ""`.
- `speck review` can now exit with code 12 when a round is still running after 9 minutes. The build skill handles it by running `speck review <id> --wait` until it exits with another code, so if you run reviews by hand, do the same.
- A panel page that's already open notices the new page, and its Foreman's notice board says Reload page: reload it to see the changes.

See everything that's changed in the [0.13.0 section of the changelog](https://github.com/luroai/speck/blob/v0.13.0/CHANGELOG.md#0130--22-october-2026).
