0.16.0 is a minor release that gives speck something to watch. While builds run, speck now stands beside a little retro TV for each build and does something different every few seconds, from ticking off its checklist and eating popcorn to playing Pong and a drumroll, so a long build doesn't repeat itself on screen. Shipping now refuses to publish your private words or your home folder's path in a ticket, the panel can run on its own in the background, and setup ends with the panel open in front of you and `speck run` on.

## What's new

- **A TV for each build.** speck watches a little retro TV for each build, up to three, or one while a ticket ships. Each scrolls a pretend log over a progress bar, and speck hops round to look when a bar fills with a tick. The TVs are decoration, not a build's real progress.
- **21 things to do while it watches.** speck ticks off its checklist, eats popcorn, plays with a yo-yo, sneezes, nods off, gets the hiccups, is startled by a spider, balances its clipboard on its head and dances until it notices you. The nearest TV plays tricks too, such as snow, Pong, a rolling picture and a drumroll with `DRRR` and `TA-DA`. With reduced motion on, it holds one still picture.
- **Private words stay private.** `speck ship` refuses to ship a ticket whose files under `speck/` hold a word from `~/.config/speck/private-words` or your home folder's path, and names the file and line without repeating the word. speck writes paths in your home folder as `~/` paths when it records a review or a build in a ticket. When `speck run` ships it, the ticket goes back to its build to take them out.
- **The panel in the background.** `speck panel --background` starts the panel on its own and gives you your terminal back, and `speck panel --stop` stops it.
- **Setup opens the panel.** Setup finishes with the panel running and open, in your agent's own browser when it has one, and `speck run` on, so a ticket starts building as soon as you set it ready.
- **Fewer stale warnings.** A "Merged, but not pushed" warning goes away once the remote has the ticket marked as done, even after a history rewrite.

## Update

Paste the setup prompt from the README into your coding agent, or run this in each project's folder:

```sh
npx github:luroai/speck#v0.16.0 init
```

## Upgrading from 0.15.0

- Run `npx github:luroai/speck#v0.16.0 init` again in each project to update the speck command and skills. It keeps your config and tickets.
- `speck ship` now refuses a ticket whose files under `speck/` hold one of your private words or your home folder's path. Take them out, writing home folder paths as `~/` paths, and ship again.

See everything that's changed in the [0.16.0 section of the changelog](https://github.com/luroai/speck/blob/v0.16.0/CHANGELOG.md#0160--25-october-2026).
