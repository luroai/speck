1.3.0 is the release where speck picks the crew for each ticket. Not every ticket needs the same builder: a one-line fix can go quickly at low effort, a hard one deserves the most effort, and some work suits a particular model. A ticket can now be built at a level, such as quick, balanced, deep or one you add yourself, which you set up on the Builder's card in Settings and pick for each ticket in the panel, or on a model and effort of its own. Leave it blank and it builds on your settings, as before.

## What's new

- **Build levels.** `levels:` in `speck/config.yml` names levels, each a saved builder, model and effort. Every project starts with `quick` (low effort), `balanced` (your settings as they are) and `deep` (maximum effort), and you can add your own, such as one for graphics work on a particular model. So you choose how hard a ticket is worked in a word, rather than in model names.
- **Levels on the team page.** The Builder's card in Settings lists the levels, and you change, add and remove them there, saved straight away. A level that a ticket not yet shipped or cancelled names can't be removed, and the card says which tickets name it. So you can tune them from your phone.
- **Pick a ticket's level in the panel.** A ticket's facts show its Level beside its Priority. Press it to pick another, or Settings to take it off, on any ticket that hasn't started building. So setting a level is as quick as setting a priority.
- **A model of its own.** A ticket can name its own `builder_model` and `builder_effort`. Every build of it runs on them, including a resume after a stop and a hand-back after a refused ship. So one ticket can build on one model and the next on another, without changing your settings in between.
- **speck fixes main itself.** With `watch` set to fix or decide, when the full test suite fails on `main`, speck makes a fix ticket naming the failing tests, sets it ready and builds it next. Build shows it's fixing it, and nothing waits under Needs you unless the fix gets stuck. So a broken `main` no longer needs you to notice it and write the ticket.

## What's changed

- **An open draft in Plan** now shows seven facts, with Level among them, so Type sits on a row of its own.
- **The Full suite fails view** sets its facts a touch smaller, at the panel's usual size for small details.

The shipped price table has this release's rates. Costs speck works out from tokens are still estimates at API list prices, so they can differ a little from what you were billed.

## Update

Paste the setup prompt from the README into your coding agent, or run this in each project's folder:

```sh
npx github:luroai/speck#v1.3.0 init
```

## Upgrading from 1.2.0

- Run `npx github:luroai/speck#v1.3.0 init` again in each project to update the speck command and skills. It keeps your config and tickets.
- Tickets get three new lines: `level`, `builder_model` and `builder_effort`. New tickets have them blank, and older tickets without them count as blank. Blank means the ticket builds on your settings, exactly as in 1.2.0. For each of the builder, model and effort, a ticket's own value wins over its level's, and its level's over your settings'.
- A project without `levels:` in `speck/config.yml` has the three default levels, `quick`, `balanced` and `deep`, without your having to add them. Your first change to a level on the Builder's card writes all three into `levels:` with it. Once `levels:` is there, the project has exactly the levels it lists.
- `speck check` now refuses a ticket that names a level the project doesn't have, or an effort it would refuse in your settings, and applies the same-model rule to the model a ticket's own value or its level gives it.
- With `watch` set to fix or decide, speck now makes and readies a fix ticket by itself when the full suite fails on `main`, so a priority-0 ticket can appear and build without you. With `watch` set to tell or off, a failing full suite waits under Needs you, as in 1.2.0.

See everything that's changed in the [1.3.0 section of the changelog](https://github.com/luroai/speck/blob/v1.3.0/CHANGELOG.md#130--12-november-2026).
