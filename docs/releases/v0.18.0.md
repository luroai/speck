0.18.0 gives ideas a lane of their own. The panel now has a Capture lane, left of Plan, holding every idea and inbox note, so Plan holds only tickets. Ideas an agent came up with are labelled as an agent's, so you can tell them from yours at a glance. And every closed card is now the same height, so the lanes line up neatly on a phone.

## What's new

- **A Capture lane.** Ideas and inbox notes have moved out of Plan into a new lane on the far left, all in one list, newest first. Before, Plan's Tickets, Ideas and Inbox buttons took a whole row at the top of the lane and showed only one list at a time. Capture is for reading: tap a note to see it in full, and change it by talking to your agent as before.
- **Plan holds only tickets.** With the buttons gone, Plan's draft tickets start straight under its title, leaving more room on a small screen.
- **Your ideas and an agent's.** An idea an agent raised by itself, such as a follow-up from a build, has a small `agent` tag on its card in Capture. Your own ideas have none. Before, both sat in the same list with nothing to tell them apart.
- **Cards that line up.** Every closed card in Plan, Build and Shipped is the same height, with its title and second line each on one line. A closed Plan card's second line is now just its priority, size, risk and type as small labels, and its criteria count and lint result show when you open it. Before, a long details line could wrap and make one card taller than the rest.

## Update

Paste the setup prompt from the README into your coding agent, or run this in each project's folder:

```sh
npx github:luroai/speck#v0.18.0 init
```

## Upgrading from 0.17.0

- Run `npx github:luroai/speck#v0.18.0 init` again in each project to update the speck command and skills. It keeps your config and tickets.
- Ideas and the inbox have moved from Plan to the new Capture lane, left of Plan. Swipe right from Plan, or tap Capture in the strip, to find them. The panel still opens on Build.
- New idea notes have a `Raised by:` line. Notes you already have need no change: one with a `From ticket:` line counts as an agent's, and any other as yours.
- There are no new settings, so nothing needs adding to `speck/config.yml`.

See everything that's changed in the [0.18.0 section of the changelog](https://github.com/luroai/speck/blob/v0.18.0/CHANGELOG.md#0180--26-october-2026).
