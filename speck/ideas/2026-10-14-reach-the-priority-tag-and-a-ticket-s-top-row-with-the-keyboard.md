# Reach the priority tag and a ticket's top row with the keyboard

Captured: 2026-10-14 · Status: inbox
Triaged: 1
From ticket: 0161

## What
Make a draft's priority tag, and the top row that opens and closes a ticket, idea or inbox note in Plan and Build, reachable with Tab and usable with Enter and Space, as buttons are.

## Why
0161 gave every button, link and tab a clear focus ring, but these two controls take only a click, so someone who uses the keyboard can't open a ticket in the lists or set a draft's priority.
