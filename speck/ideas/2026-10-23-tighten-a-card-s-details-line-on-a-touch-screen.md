# Tighten a card's details line on a touch screen

Captured: 2026-10-23 · Status: inbox
From ticket: 0419

## What
On a touch screen, a Plan or Build card's details line (0419) is 44 px a line, with wide gaps either side of each " · ", so each tag keeps its 44 px tap area (0417). A phone's cards are much taller than a mouse's, and a line that wraps leaves big gaps. Find a layout that keeps the tap areas apart but is closer to the two-line look.

## Why
Someone using the panel on a phone or tablet gets tall, sparse cards, so few fit on screen: the problem 0419 fixed for a mouse.
