# Shorter ticket view, with the full ticket on demand

Captured: 2026-10-09 · Status: ticketed (0054)

## What
Change the expanded ticket view in the panel's Shaping area to show only the problem, outcome and readiness pane. To see the whole ticket, click a button that opens it in a popup or side pane.

## Why
An open ticket shows everything (problem, outcome, acceptance criteria, out of scope and notes), which makes it long. A short view lets the user judge a ticket at a glance, with the full detail one click away.
