# Drop the chain and padlock icons from closed cards

Captured: 2026-10-24 · Status: ticketed (0483)
From ticket: 0468

## What
A closed Plan or Build card shows a chain or padlock icon after its id and, since 0468, a dependency chip at the end of its title row. Show only the chip on a closed card, and keep the icons for open cards if they still help there.

## Why
The two say the same thing twice, and the chain icon after the id looks like the chip's link icon while meaning the opposite: the chain icon means others wait on this ticket, the chip's link icon means this ticket waits on others. Someone scanning the board can read them the wrong way round, and on a phone the extra icon takes room from the title.
