# Don't say Ready to ship while the base ticket hasn't shipped

Captured: 2026-11-15 21:58 · Status: ticketed (0709)
From ticket: 0698
Raised by: agent

## What
An approved ticket built on another ticket that hasn't shipped yet should not read "Ready to ship" under Needs you. It should say it waits for that ticket to ship first, and only ask the user once it can really ship.

## Why
Its auto-ship is off until the base ticket ships, so the panel puts it under Needs you as Ready to ship, though shipping it now would have to wait. The user is asked to act when there's nothing to do yet.
