# Keep a closed card's bug or document tag whole on a phone

Captured: 2026-10-24 · Status: ticketed (0484)
From ticket: 0443

## What
On a phone, a closed Build ticket with a long status line, such as `Foreman will answer · Review 2 · 99:59 · bug`, cuts its bug, fix or document tag off with an ellipsis, sometimes to just `…`. Show the tag whole, or leave it off a closed card when it won't fit.

## Why
A lone `…` after the time looks like the time itself is cut off, and the tag it hides can't be read or tapped until the card is opened.
