# Show a cut-off details line whole in its tooltip

Captured: 2026-11-10 07:54 · Status: ticketed (0615)
From ticket: 0599
Raised by: agent

## What
Since 0599 a closed row's details line stays on one line and is cut off with an ellipsis when it doesn't fit. A Shipped row's details have a tooltip, but a Build or Plan row's don't, so the end of a long line (such as a long "after 0599, 0600, …" list) can only be read by opening the ticket. Give a cut-off details line its whole text as its tooltip.

## Why
On a phone, someone scanning Build can't see every ticket a queued one waits on without opening it.

## Notes
- The builder's own note while building 0599. A Build row's line has a tooltip only when its sentence differs from what it says.
