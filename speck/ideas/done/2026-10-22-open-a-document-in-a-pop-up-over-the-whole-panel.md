# Open a document in a pop-up over the whole panel

Captured: 2026-10-22 · Status: ticketed (0362, 0363)

## What
When you open a document task's Markdown document, show it in a pop-up over the whole panel window, not in the pane on the right-hand side.

## Why
Opening the document in the side pane is nice, but it could be better. The pane is at most 480 px wide, which is narrow for reading a document. A pop-up over everything gives it room and matches the notice board's pop-up.
