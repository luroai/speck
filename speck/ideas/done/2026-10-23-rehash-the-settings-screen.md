# Rehash the Settings screen

Captured: 2026-10-23 · Status: ticketed (0395, 0396, 0397)

## What
Make Settings simpler to read and use: explanations that close when you click or tap outside them, Appearance and Auto-build at the top as toggles without descriptions, and no "repo", "repo default" or "this machine" marks or "What speck uses now" paragraph. Where a setting is saved goes into its explanation instead.

## Why
The user, 2026-10-23: "I think we need a complete rehash of the settings screen." An open explanation stays until it's dismissed on purpose, which on a phone means tapping its button again. The scope marks and the paragraph at the top confuse more than they explain, and "speck run" isn't an obvious name for the Auto-build switch.
