# Keep the review pack's Result column readable

Captured: 2026-10-23 · Status: ticketed (0444)
From ticket: 0423

## What
In a review pack's acceptance criteria table, the Result column is squeezed to one letter wide, so its heading reads one letter per line ("R e s u l t") and the table's header row is very tall. The column should be wide enough for its heading and its mark.

## Why
It makes the top of every review pack's criteria table hard to read, for anyone checking a ticket before shipping it.

## Notes
- Seen in the builder's own pictures for 0423, in a review pack at 1280 px wide. It was there before 0423: the table's columns share out the width by their text, and the long Criterion and Evidence columns take nearly all of it.
- 0424 changes the text's sizes and spacing, but not the table's columns.
