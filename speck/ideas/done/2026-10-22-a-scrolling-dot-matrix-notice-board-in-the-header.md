# A scrolling dot-matrix notice board in the header

Captured: 2026-10-22 · Status: ticketed (0365, 0366)

## What
Replace the building/status button at the top of the panel (the Foreman's button) with an old-school LCD notice board. It scrolls words from right to left in the dot-matrix style and gives a little overview. Clicking it opens a pop-up over everything that shows the full text.

## Why
Today clicking the status button opens its detail at the top of the panel, which then scrolls away with the page. A notice board would give an overview at a glance, and a pop-up would keep the detail in view and on top.
