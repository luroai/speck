# Keep the document pop-up's Close button in view

Captured: 2026-10-22 · Status: ticketed (0379)
From ticket: 0362

## What
Keep the document pop-up's path and Close button at the top of the window while a long document scrolls under them.

## Why
On a phone there's no Escape key, so someone partway down a long document has to scroll all the way back up to close it.
