Captured: 2026-10-24 · Status: inbox
Triaged: 1

## What
Before 0462, an open Build ticket's priority, size and risk were tags that showed what they mean when tapped (for example "Low risk: ships by itself once approved"). In the facts grid they're plain values, whose tooltip is only their own text. Give the cells a way to show their meaning again.
