Captured: 2026-10-24 · Status: inbox

## What
A shipped ticket's full view shows its figures but not whether it was a bug fix, a fix ticket or a document. Its card shows that as a small tag beside its title. Show it in the full view too, for example beside the title or as a cell in its table.

## Why
